### 3. Key Solution Highlights (For Submission Text Fields)

- **Platform Overview:** Project Udyam Digital is an AI-first acquisition, automated KYC, and self-service onboarding engine for India's 6 Crore+ MSMEs.
- **Multilingual AI Advisor:** Voice & text assistant supporting English, Hindi, and Tamil with plain-language business rationales.
- **Transparent Quote Builder:** Real-time pricing across 25 products with 1-click presets for Kiranas, Clinics, Multi-store chains, and Manufacturers.
- **Smart KYC Validator:** Automated document inspection handling all 12 hackathon test specimens (K01–K12) with instant Mod-36 checksum, blur/skew detection, and registry cross-verification.
- **Partner CRM & ML Propensity:** Random Forest machine learning model scoring 5,000 SMB leads with prescriptive Next-Best-Action guidance.
- **Business Impact:**
  - Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) down from ₹15,000 to **₹2,450 (-83.7%)**
  - Lead-to-activation cycle down from 17.6 days to **< 24–72 hours**
  - Funnel conversion up from 7.36% to **28.0% (3.8x surge)**
  - Customer follow-up calls reduced from 3.0 to **0**
